What we build

Automation with a human holding the pen.

Everything we build follows one rule: the software drafts and prepares, people decide and approve. That is true of our own product, InspectAI, and it is true of the systems we build for clients.

01 · The systems

Where the hours go.

Estimating + intake

Quotes in minutes, not evenings

Customers arrive with photos and half a description. We build the intake that asks for the right details up front and the drafting that turns them into a quote a human can review in minutes.

Follow-up + pipeline

No lead dies in silence

Most jobs are lost after the estimate, in the quiet. Every lead tracked, every conversation in one thread, reminders that go out on time because no one had to remember them.

Scheduling + payments

Booked and paid, cleanly

Booking that respects the business's real availability, payment links on approved work, and customer records that stay organized on their own. The unglamorous systems that decide whether a business feels calm or chaotic.

02 · Custom builds

For businesses that are serious.

When a business is serious about fixing a workflow, we build their version of it: scoped to the bottleneck, built on the same patterns as our own products, and delivered as working software, not a demo that dies in a slide deck. Some of these builds become products. All of them have to earn their keep in the first weeks.
