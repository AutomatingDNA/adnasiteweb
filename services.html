What we build

Automation with a human holding the pen.

Everything we build follows one rule: the software drafts and prepares, people decide and approve. That is true of our own product, InspectAI, and it is true of the systems we build for clients.

Estimating and intake automation

Customers arrive with photos and half a description. We build the intake that asks for the right details up front and the drafting that turns them into a quote a human can review in minutes, not an evening.

Follow-up and pipeline systems

Most jobs are lost after the estimate, in the silence. We build the pipeline and the follow-up rhythm: every lead tracked, every conversation in one thread, reminders that go out on time because no one had to remember them.

Scheduling, payments, and records

Booking that respects the business's real availability, payment links on approved work, and customer records that stay organized on their own. The unglamorous systems that decide whether a business feels calm or chaotic.

Custom builds for businesses

When a business is serious about fixing a workflow, we build their version of it: scoped to the bottleneck, built on the same patterns as our own products, and delivered as software they own the use of, not a demo that dies in a slide deck. Some of these builds become products. All of them have to earn their keep in the first weeks.

Conversation first

Built in the open
