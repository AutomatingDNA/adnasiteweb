03 · How we work

Automation with a human holding the pen.

Find the real bottleneck

We start with the workflow as it actually runs, not as it is supposed to run. The expensive step is usually quoting or follow-up, and it is usually hiding in plain sight.

Build the smallest useful system

Software earns its keep in the first week or it becomes shelfware. We build the narrow version that removes the pain, put it in front of real users, and let their behavior decide what comes next.

Keep humans in charge of the money

Our AI drafts, suggests, and prepares. People review and approve anything a customer sees or pays. That rule is designed into InspectAI, and it is how we build for clients too.
