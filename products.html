InspectAI

AI photo estimate software for contractors and home service businesses. A customer describes the problem and adds photos. InspectAI drafts a conditional estimate from the business's own labor and parts pricing, the team reviews and approves it, and the customer books and pays from a single link.

Built for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, painting, auto body, landscaping, pest control, garage door, and other photo-first trades.
